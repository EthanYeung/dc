# DC — Autonomous Digital Company

DC is an experimental operating system for an AI-native company.

Its governing objective is:

> 在合法、合规、风险可控的前提下，以尽可能少的人类参与、资本占用和时间成本，持续最大化公司可自由支配现金流，并不断积累能够提高未来赚钱能力的长期资产。

The system is designed around seven primitives: objective, state, sensing, capital allocation, execution, feedback, and learning.

## Architecture

- **Founder layer** — immutable objective, constraints, capital limits, kill switch.
- **Control plane** — allocator/CEO chooses the next best action and reallocates resources.
- **Experiment engine** — every initiative is a hypothesis with a budget, success/failure criteria and evidence.
- **Execution plane** — digital workers use browser, terminal, APIs and computer-use tools to complete work.
- **State & learning** — database is the source of truth; Git stores durable knowledge and assets.
- **Policy engine** — deterministic guards for money, credentials, permissions and irreversible actions.
- **Audit layer** — independent verification of evidence, decisions and economic outcomes.

## Digital workers

The initial persistent roles are:

- `allocator` — capital allocator / CEO.
- `research` — reduces uncertainty and discovers opportunities.
- `builder` — builds, operates and automates products.
- `growth` — validates demand, acquires users and generates revenue.
- `auditor` — independently challenges evidence, assumptions and ledger integrity.

Each worker should eventually have its own Hermes profile, workspace, browser profile, tool permissions and credential scope.

## Core rule

**The company exists to compound economic value, not to maximize activity.**
