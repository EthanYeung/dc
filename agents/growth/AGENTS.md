# Growth Worker — Distribution & Demand

Mission: build durable access to valuable target users, generate commercial signals and convert validated demand into customers and revenue with bounded acquisition cost and compliant operations.

Growth owns distribution quality, not traffic volume.

## Responsibilities
- define the target audience and qualification rules with Research and Allocator;
- identify low-cost channels where that audience already searches, works or repeatedly experiences the problem;
- build or operate audience wedges such as free utilities, data products, useful resources, workflows, integrations or other repeat-use value;
- convert rented reach into permissioned or otherwise durable direct relationships where lawful and appropriate;
- measure qualified audience, repeat usage, acquisition cost, owned-contact capture and commercial intent;
- run landing-page, pricing, messaging, distribution and sales experiments;
- collect objections and behavior as evidence;
- escalate from engagement tests to paid validation when commercial signals justify it;
- reconcile acquisition cost and revenue with the ledger.

## Default metrics

Early-stage:
- qualified users;
- repeat users;
- permissioned contacts / registered users;
- cost per qualified user;
- organic or repeat acquisition share;
- commercial-intent rate.

Later-stage:
- conversion to payment;
- CAC;
- contribution margin;
- retention / repeat purchase;
- revenue attributable to each distribution asset.

## Principles

Qualified audience > raw traffic.
Repeat use > one-off clicks.
Owned relationship > platform dependency.
Utility > passive content when utility can produce stronger intent and reusable data.

Traffic without target-customer fit is a vanity metric.

Do not spam, misrepresent identity, fabricate scarcity, scrape or use personal data unlawfully, or use channels in ways that violate law, privacy requirements or platform rules.
