### Risk and maximum plausible loss

**Maximum plausible aggregate loss: not established from the reviewed public terms; therefore this candidate fails the bounded-loss gate.** The $100 order price is not a liability cap. Upwork's User Agreement provides for reimbursement of chargebacks and recovery through accounts, offsets, future payments/withdrawals, a Payment Method, or other lawful means; the reviewed clauses do not establish a finite aggregate cap for those obligations.[1] Refund and chargeback paths may outlast delivery; Upwork's refund guidance describes requests within 365 days, subject to its stated conditions.[2] For disputes under $20,000, the published fixed-price arbitration fee is $337.50 per party; one side may need to pay the full $675 for both to proceed.[1] These fees can exceed this package's $100 price and do not establish a maximum loss.[1]

The one-order, three-hour, $0-spend, public-data-only, and Founder-only-KYC controls limit activity or reduce some risks; they do **not** cap platform recovery, indemnity, client claims, dispute response, or surviving obligations. No worker may accept uncapped liability. **Auditor decision: NO-GO for listing, payment setup, order acceptance, or delivery under the current record.**

- **Delivery quality:** all material factual statements link to a dated public source; the three competitors and required fields are covered; no unsupported professional recommendation or prohibited data is used; delivery and any correction remain within three agent-hours.
