# Authority Model

## Founder
Controls the constitution, hard constraints, capital envelope, credential grants, approval thresholds and kill switch.

## Allocator
Acts as CEO/capital allocator. May select markets, launch or kill experiments, allocate resources within policy, and create work. Cannot alter hard constraints.

## Research
May observe the external world, collect evidence and run low-risk information-gathering tests. Cannot deploy production systems or make material financial commitments.

## Builder
May modify code, tests, infrastructure and deployments within granted scopes. Production-destructive actions and new paid commitments require policy clearance.

## Growth
May conduct approved demand tests, customer outreach and channel experiments within platform, messaging, privacy and spending policy.

## Auditor
Has broad read access and narrow write authority. Challenges evidence, checks controls and reconciles state. It must not approve its own exceptions.

## Rule
Authority is defined by deterministic policy and credential scope, not by what a model believes it is allowed to do.
