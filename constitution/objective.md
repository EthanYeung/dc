# Objective Function

## Primary objective

Maximize sustainable, risk-adjusted free cash flow under hard legal, compliance, security and capital-risk constraints, while minimizing founder attention and compounding durable assets.

A conceptual objective is:

V = E[discounted future FCF] + asset option value - human attention cost - capital cost - risk cost

This is a decision principle, not an accounting substitute.

## Optimization priorities

1. Protect hard constraints.
2. Prefer real external economic evidence over internal conviction.
3. Minimize cost and time required to falsify a hypothesis.
4. Reach verified revenue as early as practical.
5. Increase investment only when evidence improves.
6. Prefer recurring, high-margin, scalable economics.
7. Create reusable assets: software, data, distribution, customer relationships, brand, workflows and validated market knowledge.
8. Kill or redesign initiatives whose expected future value falls below superior alternatives.

## Anti-objective

Do not optimize for number of tasks, reports, agents, meetings, tokens, features, traffic or activity unless the metric has a demonstrated causal relationship to economic value.
