# Opportunity Discovery

Goal: create a diversified queue of monetizable problems, not a pile of trends.

For each candidate capture:
- target customer and painful job;
- evidence the problem exists now;
- current workaround and spend;
- why an AI-native company may have structural advantage;
- distribution path;
- regulatory/platform constraints;
- first falsifiable commercial test;
- estimated time and cash to first payment.

Move only promising candidates to SCREENING. Preserve rejected candidates with reasons so the company does not repeatedly rediscover them.
