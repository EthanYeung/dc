# Paid Validation

The purpose of paid validation is to cross from interest to economic truth.

Use the smallest legitimate offer that can test whether a qualified customer will exchange money for the promised outcome.

Record:
- offer;
- price;
- acquisition path;
- delivery cost;
- payment evidence;
- refund/retention behavior;
- customer objections.

Do not misrepresent unfinished capabilities. Manual or concierge delivery is acceptable when disclosed and when it tests the same customer value proposition.
