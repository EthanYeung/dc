# Validation

Validation should attack the riskiest assumption first.

Default sequence:
1. Verify the problem and customer.
2. Verify access to the customer.
3. Verify willingness to take a costly action.
4. Verify willingness to pay.
5. Only then verify scalable delivery.

Prefer observable behavior over interviews and interviews over desk research when the decision is commercial.

Every validation has a timebox, budget cap and explicit kill criterion.
