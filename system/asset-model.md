# Asset Model

## Principle

An asset is something created or acquired by the company that can improve future earning power beyond the immediate task that produced it.

A completed task is not automatically an asset.

## Core asset classes

### DistributionAsset
Durable access to a valuable audience:
- repeat-use utility;
- direct permissioned audience;
- organic acquisition surface;
- channel partnership;
- workflow integration;
- reusable discovery surface.

### SoftwareAsset
Reusable code, service, automation, integration or internal operating system.

### DataAsset
Legally obtained, reusable proprietary or structured data with continuing decision or product value.

### BrandAsset
Recognizable trust, reputation, domain authority or identity that measurably improves acquisition, conversion or retention.

### WorkflowAsset
A repeatable process/playbook/automation that reduces future cost, time or error.

### CustomerRelationship
A legitimate direct commercial relationship, account, contract or repeat buyer connection.

### KnowledgeAsset
Validated market knowledge, evidence corpus, benchmark, model or decision rule that can materially improve future decisions.

## Required asset dimensions

Each material asset should be evaluable across:

### Identity
- id;
- type;
- name;
- status.

### Ownership and provenance
- owning venture, if any;
- linked opportunity;
- source experiment;
- creating worker;
- artifact/location.

### Economics
- build/acquisition cost;
- ongoing maintenance cost;
- attributable revenue or cost reduction where measurable;
- replacement cost.

### Durability
- owned / rented / hybrid;
- external platform dependency;
- expected decay or obsolescence;
- portability;
- reuse scope across ventures.

### Evidence
- usage;
- qualified users;
- repeat use;
- commercial signals;
- revenue / savings;
- supporting evidence references.

## Asset quality

Prefer assets that:
- remain useful after the original experiment ends;
- have low marginal maintenance cost;
- can serve multiple future products or ventures;
- reduce dependence on a single external platform;
- improve future distribution, data, execution speed or cash generation.

Do not inflate asset value merely because work was expensive to produce.

## Relationship to ventures

Opportunities are hypotheses about economic value.

Ventures are active operating bets.

Assets may belong to:
- one venture;
- multiple ventures;
- the company platform itself.

Cross-venture reusable assets should be explicitly marked because they can change capital-allocation economics.
