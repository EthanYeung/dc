# Evidence Model

Evidence levels:

0. Agent opinion or unsupported inference.
1. Secondary research from attributable sources.
2. Observed user or market behavior.
3. Qualified lead, signup or comparable costly signal.
4. Explicit commercial commitment or LOI.
5. Verified payment.
6. Repeat payment / retention.
7. Replicable acquisition and positive unit economics.

Each evidence record should contain provenance, timestamp, linked hypothesis, confidence, raw artifact location and verification status.

Higher evidence levels dominate larger volumes of low-level opinion when allocating capital.
